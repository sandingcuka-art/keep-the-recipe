# Keep the Recipe

Keep the Recipe is a baking app for people who love creating, saving, and scaling recipes. It gives you a place to keep your own recipes, save ideas you want to try, and organize everything in one beautiful, easy-to-use space.

## Why this app?

Baking is personal, creative, and often full of trial and error. Whether you are making a family cake, testing a new dessert, or saving a recipe from a cookbook or social media, it can be hard to keep track of what worked, what needs adjusting, and how to scale a recipe for a bigger crowd.

Keep the Recipe helps bakers:

- save recipes they have created
- save recipes they want to try later
- organize favorite desserts, cakes, breads, and cookies
- upload photos of the final result or inspiration
- scale ingredients for different serving sizes
- get smart help adjusting recipes based on the number of people they want to serve

## Core idea

This app is designed for home bakers and baking enthusiasts who want a safe, simple place to store recipes and inspiration. You can keep your favorite family recipes, add your own custom creations, and save recipes you discover online or in cookbooks.

## Key features

### Recipe storage
Users can create and save recipes with:

- recipe title
- short description
- ingredients
- method or instructions
- prep and bake time
- servings
- notes about what worked well

### Image uploads
Users can upload pictures for:

- the finished baked item
- the recipe inspiration
- step-by-step baking stages
- their own kitchen creations

This makes the app more visual and helps bakers remember what a recipe looked like when it was successful.

### Safe personal recipe library
The app should feel personal and safe, like a private space for your baking ideas and treasured recipes. It should store your custom recipes securely and make it easy to revisit them whenever you need them.

### Smart recipe scaling assistant
This is the feature that makes the app feel extra helpful.

A user can upload a recipe they want to make, then ask an agent to calculate the measurements for a different serving size. For example:

- “I want this cake to feed 12 people.”
- “This recipe is for 4 servings.”
- “Can you scale this so it serves 12?”

The app can then calculate the adjusted ingredient amounts automatically and tell the user something like:

> To feed 12 people, multiply all ingredients by 3. For example, the original recipe uses 200g flour for 4 people, so for 12 people you need 600g flour.

This makes baking for gatherings, birthdays, events, and larger families much easier.

## Example of the scaling feature

A user uploads a recipe like:

- Chocolate cake
- Serves 4
- 200g flour
- 100g sugar
- 2 eggs
- 50g cocoa powder

Then they ask: “Can you make this serve 12?”

The agent calculates:

- 200g flour × 3 = 600g flour
- 100g sugar × 3 = 300g sugar
- 2 eggs × 3 = 6 eggs
- 50g cocoa powder × 3 = 150g cocoa powder

The app could then show the adjusted recipe clearly and help the user bake the right amount without doing all the math manually.

## What this app is about

Keep the Recipe is for people who love baking, want to remember their best recipes, and need a smarter way to adjust ingredients for different group sizes. It combines the joy of creativity with the practicality of a organized recipe planner.
